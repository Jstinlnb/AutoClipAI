AutoClipAI Privacy Policy

AutoClipAI processes video content supplied by the user in order to create short-form video clips.

When a user connects TikTok, YouTube or another supported service, AutoClipAI may receive authorization tokens and basic account information required to provide upload functionality.

Authentication credentials are used only to provide the requested platform integration. AutoClipAI does not sell personal information.

Video files, transcripts and generated clips may be processed and stored locally on the user's computer.

Connected third-party services such as TikTok and YouTube are also governed by their own privacy policies.

Users may revoke platform access at any time through the relevant platform account settings.
